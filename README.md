Entry for the [make.fun bounty: The Excuse That Almost Works](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) — one line, at most 20 words, the text a gamer sends the boss to skip work during GTA 6 launch week. It has to read like a real hurried text, almost believable to a tired manager.

## The excuse

> Sorry, courier won't leave my launch-day delivery without a signature, stuck at home till noon.

15 words. 95 characters. One line. No setup, no explanation, no signature — just the text as sent.

## Why this line

The trick is the boring detail: a courier who needs a signature is the most ordinary obstacle in the world, and a tired manager has heard it a hundred times — usually when it is true. The only wink is "launch-day delivery", which the manager can read straight as any delivery while the whole office knows exactly which box it is. The excuse almost works because it is technically true.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the excuse above, and also writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set), as the bounty harness requires.
